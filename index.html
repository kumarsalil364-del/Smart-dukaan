SMART DUKAAN

Complete Shop Management Website

Act as a professional full-stack web developer and create a complete, modern, responsive and fully functional shop management website named SMART DUKAAN.

1. Technology Requirements

  • Use HTML5, CSS3 and JavaScript.
  • Create the complete application in one index.html file.
  • Use LocalStorage to save data in the browser.
  • Make the website compatible with GitHub Pages.
  • All buttons, forms, menus and features must work.
  • Provide complete code, not placeholders or examples.

2. Design and Branding

  • Create a professional SMART DUKAAN logo.
  • Use a premium blue, navy and teal colour theme.
  • Create a modern dashboard and sidebar navigation.
  • Add attractive cards, icons and smooth animations.
  • Make the website responsive for mobile, tablet and desktop.

3. Dashboard

  • Today's sales and total sales.
  • Today's expenses and total expenses.
  • Estimated profit after expenses.
  • Total products and stock value.
  • Low-stock alerts.
  • Recent sales and expense records.
  • Automatically update all dashboard figures.

4. Firm Profile

  • Business name and owner name.
  • Shop address and phone number.
  • Email address and GSTIN.
  • Business logo.
  • Allow editing and saving firm details.
  • Display firm information on invoices.

5. Product and Inventory Management

  • Add, edit and delete products.
  • Product name, ID, category and description.
  • Purchase price and selling price.
  • Available quantity and minimum stock level.
  • GST percentage.
  • Search and filter products.
  • Automatically reduce stock after completed sales.
  • Show low-stock alerts.
  • Prevent sales when stock is insufficient.

6. Multiple Product Billing System

  • Generate automatic invoice numbers.
  • Enter customer name and phone number.
  • Add multiple products to one bill.
  • Enter a separate quantity for each product.
  • Automatically calculate individual subtotals.
  • Calculate GST and final bill amount.
  • Allow removing products from the bill.
  • Save completed bills.
  • Update stock after successful sales.
  • Print professional invoices.

15. Important Functional Requirements

  • All calculations must be accurate.
  • Validate all form inputs.
  • Do not accept invalid negative prices or quantities.
  • Update stock correctly after every completed sale.
  • Update dashboard figures automatically.
  • Ask for confirmation before deleting records.
  • Preserve data after browser refresh.
  • Show useful success and error messages.
  • Handle empty records without crashing.
  • Clearly distinguish estimated profit from actual accounting profit.
  • Credit sales must not be counted as collected payments.
